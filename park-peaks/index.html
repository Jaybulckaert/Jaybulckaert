What is actually moving

The photo was split into four plates. The back plate is the untouched photograph. Two mountain plates hold the range on the left and the peak on the right, each cut along its ridge against the sky, with rock and slope reconstructed behind the trees so no hole opens when they move. The front plate is everything nearer than the mountains: every tree, the kiosk, the cars, the house, the lawn, and you.

magnification = scale / 1400 each mountain = scaled about its own base under its summit back and front = never transformed

Each plate pivots on its own base line, where the mountain disappears behind the treeline, so both peaks grow in place instead of sliding apart. Past about 2.5× the two flanks meet behind the trees and overlap, the way ranges do.

The cut was made with colour-model segmentation seeded by hand-traced ridge lines. The ridges are accurate to a pixel or two at half resolution. The slopes hidden behind the trees are reconstructed, and that reconstruction is what you see directly above the treeline at high magnification. The 1400 : 1 label is your reading of the frame and the metre figures follow from it.
